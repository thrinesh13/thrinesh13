### [Operating Room Scheduling Reliability & Cost Exposure](https://github.com/thrinesh13/OR-Scheduling-Reliability-Analytics)

**End-to-End BI & Analytics | Source Data → Data Quality → Modeling → Power BI Reporting**

`Microsoft Fabric` `PySpark` `Spark SQL` `ETL/ELT` `Data Quality` `Star Schema` `Semantic Model` `DAX` `Power BI` `KPI Development`

Owned the analytical workflow from raw perioperative source files to decision-ready reporting. Ingested and transformed **1.9M+ records** through a Bronze/Silver/Gold medallion architecture, profiled and resolved timestamp and data-quality issues, and produced **48,118 curated surgical cases across 418 procedures**. Built a star-schema analytical model and Power BI semantic layer with reusable DAX measures and KPIs to evaluate scheduling reliability and operational performance.

**Business outcome:** Quantified a **54.1-minute mean absolute scheduling difference** and estimated **$15.8M–$27.1M in annual gross time-cost exposure** under documented planning scenarios, translating technical analysis into operational findings that can be understood by technical and non-technical audiences.

**Recruiter signal:** end-to-end data ownership · data ingestion · ETL/ELT · data profiling · data validation · transformation · data modeling · semantic modeling · KPI development · dashboard reporting · operational analytics · data storytelling · technical and non-technical communication

**Requirements & Decision Analytics | Business Question → Data Requirements → Analysis → Reporting → Recommendation**

`Requirements Gathering` `Data Requirements` `Python` `SQL` `Power BI` `Exploratory Data Analysis` `Business Rules` `KPI Definition` `Scenario Analysis` `Decision Support`

Defined the analytical requirements for a claims-processing problem, identified and gathered the data needed to evaluate those requirements, and translated the business question into measurable rules and performance indicators. Used SQL and Python to prepare and analyze the data, validate business rules, compare manual review with alternative processing scenarios, and structure the results for clear reporting and decision support.

**Business outcome:** Demonstrated how combining validation checks with risk scoring changes automation and approval quality, creating a measurable framework for comparing processing strategies and communicating the tradeoffs through KPIs and visual reporting.

### [Statistical Time-Series Analysis](https://github.com/thrinesh13/Statistical-Time-series-analysis)

**Statistical & Exploratory Analytics | EDA → Hypothesis Testing → Visualization → Interpretation**

`Python` `Pandas` `NumPy` `SciPy` `Statistical Analysis` `Hypothesis Testing` `Trend Analysis` `Correlation Analysis` `Data Visualization`

Analyzed **1,254 trading days** of Apple market data to investigate trends, relationships, volatility, and distribution behavior. Used Python for data preparation and exploratory data analysis, then applied hypothesis testing, correlation analysis, and statistical visualization to test four defined risk assumptions.

**Analytical outcome:** Rejected **3 of 4 assumptions at the 95% confidence level**, showing how statistical evidence can challenge intuitive conclusions and support more defensible interpretation of trends and risk. Presented the findings through clear visualizations and concise analytical explanations rather than statistical output alone.

**Recruiter signal:** EDA · statistical analysis · hypothesis testing · trend analysis · correlation analysis · time-series analysis · Python · data visualization · analytical reasoning · data storytelling · insight communication
